# Creative Brief Output Shape

When producing Creative Direction (design phase or internal one-shot planning), cover:

## 1. Project Identity & Core Concept
Subject, industry, emotional goal, central philosophy (one sharp sentence).

## 2. Visual Language & Color System
Palette tokens + usage rules + what to avoid.

## 3. Typography as Visual Material
Display + UI pairing, weights, scale rules, tracking, philosophy.

## 4. Layout & Composition Principles
Grid, asymmetry, focal-point rule, anti-card rule, vertical rhythm.

## 5. Motion & Cinematic Timing
Personality, easing, entry sequence, scroll behavior.

## 6. UI/UX & Interaction
Nav behavior, hover language, cursor policy, CTA style.

## 7. Image & Object Language
Ratios, grade, placement, metaphor.

## 8. Narrative Structure
Ordered acts/sections for this specific brief (not a generic template).

## 9. Anti-AI-Slop Decisions
Explicit list of rejected patterns for this project.

## 10. Why These Decisions Were Made
Short rationale table or bullets tied to the emotional goal.

## 11. Copy direction (if needed)
Overline, hero, supporting line, CTA, nav, footer tone.

- Private cinematic gallery  
- Editorial magazine × film still  
- Curated absence / restraint as identity
